How it works

  1. Type

    Enter a word or phrase, the way you would in a find bar.

  2. Point

    Aim your camera at a page, a shelf, a menu, a sign.

  3. Spot it

    Every match lights up, and stays put as you move the camera.

Features

  • Live, as you type

    No photos to take. Highlights update with every keystroke and follow the text as the camera moves.

  • Several words at once Plus

    Separate words with commas, like “milk, eggs, bread”, to find a whole list in one look.

  • Related words Plus

    Search “coffee” and also find “espresso” and “latte”, with suggestions from Apple Intelligence on your device.

  • Saved searches Plus

    Save searches you run often, and bring them back with a tap.

  • Zoom for small print

    Pinch to zoom in on text across a room or in fine print.

  • Private by design

    Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad. Nothing you see or search for leaves your device.

Cmd+F is free to download, with live search for any word or phrase. Cmd+F Plus, a one-time purchase, unlocks the features marked Plus.
